Scope Note
----------
These files are bundled solely as a DEV-only local public-fixture benchmark
evaluated at HITNet's 320x240 model resolution. Direct runtime fetch from
vision.middlebury.edu is blocked in browser previews due to absent CORS headers
and zip-only GT PFM packaging. Results are NOT an official full-resolution
Middlebury leaderboard submission.
